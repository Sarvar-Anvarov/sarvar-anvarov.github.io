Common questions

My friend and I can’t find each other for a duel

The duel connects two iPhones directly and doesn’t need the internet. Stay close to each other, and either join the same Wi-Fi network or have one of you turn on Personal Hotspot and the other join it.

Before the first search, iOS asks to let the game use the local network. If you said no, turn it back on in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › Riposte.

How do lives work?

Lives come back over time. If you don’t want to wait, you can watch a short video to get them back now. Watching is always your choice.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Yes. Progress, lives and settings are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud save, so deleting the game deletes them too.

Something I paid for is missing

Write to us with the date of the purchase and we will sort it out. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple: a refund can be requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy choices

The game only shows videos you choose to watch. Whether ads may use your advertising identifier is set in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can change your consent in the game: Settings › Privacy. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
