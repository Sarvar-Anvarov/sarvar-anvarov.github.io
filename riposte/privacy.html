Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

Riposte is made by Sarvar Anvarov, an independent developer who publishes games as Terak Studio (“we”). This policy explains what data the game and its partners handle and what choices you have. Contact: sarvar.apps@gmail.com.

What the game keeps itself

Your progress, lives and purchase status are stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud, so a new iPhone or a reinstall picks up where you left off. The iCloud copy lives in your Apple account, not with us. Settings such as language and volume stay on the device. The game has no account and no servers of ours, and we do not receive this data.

Duel with a friend

The two-player duel connects two phones directly over a local network or a personal hotspot. Fight data (moves and score) goes only between those two devices and never reaches us. iOS asks for permission to use the local network before the first search.

Ads

The game shows only rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, for extra lives or a second chance in a fight. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers: the identifier for vendors (IDFV), and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system, language and time zone;
  • ad interactions: which ads were shown, watched or tapped.

Google handles this data under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy (how Google uses data from apps).

Analytics and crash reports

To see where players get stuck and to fix crashes, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They are not used for ads and are not linked to your name or Apple account.

  • Analytics: which parts of the game you play (the lesson, the prologue, which fights you start, win, lose or leave), when lives run out, second chances, rewarded videos and purchases (never payment details). Along with these, Google receives a random app instance ID created by Firebase, device model, iOS version, language, app version and approximate location derived from the IP address. The game does not give Analytics your advertising identifier or the identifier for vendors.
  • Crash reports: when the game crashes or hits an error, the technical details of the error, device model, iOS version, free memory and disk space, whether the device is jailbroken, and random installation IDs created by Firebase.

Google keeps detailed analytics data for 2 months and crash reports for 90 days: Firebase privacy, Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking on iOS. The game asks once whether ads may use your advertising identifier. You can change the answer any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you decline, you still get ads and rewards. They are just not personalized.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. Before any ads, the game shows a consent form. Analytics stays off until then and runs only if you agree in that form. You can change your choice any time in the game: Settings › Privacy.
  • Crash reports are sent everywhere: they only help us keep the game working.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California). We do not sell your personal information for money, but under these laws showing personalized ads may count as sharing it. You can opt out any time in the game: Settings › Privacy. Opting out does not change the game: you still get ads and rewards.
  • Not watching ads is always an option: lives also come back over time.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; the game only learns that the purchase went through.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Keeping and deleting data

Analytics and crash reports are not tied to your name, so we usually cannot pick out one player's records. They are deleted automatically: analytics after 2 months, crash reports after 90 days. Deleting the game removes what it stored on your device; your iCloud copy stays in your Apple account. To erase your progress everywhere, use Settings › Start the journey over in the game. For data held by ad partners, use the links above or reset your advertising identifier in iPhone settings. If you need help with a request about your data, write to us and we will help or point you to the right partner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
